PocketPilot — Terms of Use

Version: 16 November 2026

PocketPilot is a personal, non-commercial budgeting prototype intended solely for use by the application owner with financial accounts they are authorised to access.

Purpose

The app provides budgeting, transaction categorisation and spending analysis. These features are for informational purposes only and are not financial advice. Results may be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate.

Authorisation

Financial-account connections may be initiated only with the account holder's approval. Where available, authorisation uses Enable Banking and the financial institution's own consent process. PocketPilot is designed to request account-information access only and not payment initiation.

Availability and security

PocketPilot is experimental software provided for personal use. No guarantee of continuous availability, completeness, data durability or bank compatibility is made. The owner must safeguard the device and account credentials, keep security-sensitive files private, and avoid exposing the local server publicly.

Third-party services

Banking connectivity depends on Enable Banking, participating financial institutions, the availability of their APIs and separate terms and consent processes. An account may require reauthorisation or be unavailable.

Data management

The owner is responsible for reviewing imported data, maintaining backups if needed, and revoking bank access separately when no longer required. See the PocketPilot Privacy Notice for the prototype's current data handling.

Personal-use draft. Review before publishing or sharing beyond the application owner.
