PocketPilot — Privacy Notice

Version: 16 November 2026

PocketPilot is a personal, non-commercial budgeting application intended for use by its owner only. It is not a banking service, and does not initiate payments.

Data used

When the owner chooses to connect a financial account, PocketPilot is designed to request read-only account and transaction information through Enable Banking, subject to the account holder's explicit bank authorisation. It may process account names, balances, dates, transaction descriptions and amounts. The application also stores any budgets, categories and manually entered transactions supplied by its owner.

Where information is stored

PocketPilot runs locally on the owner's computer. The existing budgeting interface stores transaction and budget information in that computer's browser local storage. Browser local storage is not encrypted. The app is being adapted for Enable Banking; do not use it with live financial accounts until the revised connector and its storage practices have been reviewed and tested. Enable Banking and the connected financial institutions process account authorisation and data according to their own privacy information.

Sharing and third parties

Account authorisation is handled via Enable Banking and the financial institution selected by the account owner. PocketPilot does not intentionally sell user data, use advertising trackers, or share account data with other users. This statement does not override the policies of the bank or Enable Banking.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete locally stored budget and transaction information using PocketPilot's local reset controls or browser data controls. Disconnecting local access is distinct from revoking consent at the bank or Enable Banking. Separate copies, such as CSV exports or backups saved by the owner, must be deleted separately.

Security

This software is a prototype for local personal use; it has not undergone independent security auditing. The owner should not publish private keys, API credentials, bank details or transaction exports. The app should not be deployed to the public internet without additional security controls.

Questions

For questions about this private project, contact the application owner through the data protection contact email provided in its Enable Banking application registration.
